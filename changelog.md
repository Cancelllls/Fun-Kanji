## 🎉 Fun Kanji Initial Release!

Welcome to **Fun Kanji**—the ultimate all-in-one Japanese writing system companion!

### 🌟 New Features & Improvements
- **Advanced Spaced Repetition (SM-2):** Maximize retention with optimized algorithms.
- **Premium Glassmorphism UI:** Stunning new design with dynamic gradients and frosted glass elements.
- **Stroke Order Practice:** Master writing with a built-in drawing canvas.
- **Custom Study Decks:** Group your characters and learn at your own pace.
- **Onyomi vs. Kunyomi Minigame:** Test your reading speed in an interactive new minigame.
- **Interactive Reading Practice:** Improve comprehension with example sentences.
- **Gamified Progression:** Earn stars and confetti bursts when you master characters.
- **Home Screen Widget:** Get your daily Kanji directly on your home screen.

*This project is a heavily modified and visually enhanced fork of the original Fun With Kanji.*
